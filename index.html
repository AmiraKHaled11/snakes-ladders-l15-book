Grade 6 - L1-5 Solve Ratio Problems - Snakes and Ladders

  1. Press ROLL: the dice tumbles and your token hops across the board.
  2. The square you land on opens a question with 4 options. Pick one.
  3. Correct: +1 point, and a ladder takes you UP, a snake takes you DOWN.
  4. Wrong: your token goes back to where it was.
  5. Use the + and - buttons beside each team to change its points. Points are saved on this device.
  6. First team to square 30 with a correct answer wins.
